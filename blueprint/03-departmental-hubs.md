## Departmental Hubs

### Accounting
- **Hub path:** `/departments/accounting/overview`
- **Purpose:** Job cost control, billing discipline, AP/AR, WIP, compliance records.
- **Key subpages:**
  1. AP & vendor management
  2. AR & progress billing
  3. Job costing & WIP
  4. Payroll/union/labor burden
  5. Sales/use tax (CA)
  6. Retainage/liens/releases
  7. Month-end close
  8. Insurance/bond/COI tracking
  9. PCard/travel expense
  10. Audit readiness/document retention
- **SOP/workflow focus:** pay-app + waiver sequencing, WIP review cadence, CO budget posting.
