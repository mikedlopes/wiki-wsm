## Implementation Roadmap & Governance

### 4-week phased rollout

#### Week 1 - Platform foundation
- Finalize Wiki.js groups and least-privilege permissions
- Configure Entra ID SSO
- Publish navigation anchors (`home`, `start-here`, `site-map`)
- Assign page owners per department

#### Week 2 - Core operating content
- Publish PM/design/field/shop/purchasing/accounting SOP baseline
- Publish project template path under `/projects/[project-id]`
- Publish standards baseline (NFPA 13/25 and CA matrix)

#### Week 3 - Dashboard and workflow integration
- Embed Planner and Power BI on projects dashboard
- Stand up SharePoint lists for submittals/RFIs/COs/material status
- Activate Power Automate notifications

#### Week 4 - Adoption + hardening
- Train users by role (office/field/leadership)
- Pilot one live TI project fully through wiki workflows
- Capture friction points and update pages
- Freeze v1 governance policy

### Content population strategy

Priority order:

1. Wayfinding and role entry pages
2. Project lifecycle and templates
3. Department SOPs
4. Standards/compliance pages
5. Archived historical material

### Training outline

- Leadership: 60 min (KPIs, governance, escalation)
- PM/Design/Purchasing: 90 min (submittals/RFIs/CO dashboards)
- Field/Shop: 60 min (daily use, mobile navigation, inspection pages)
- Accounting: 60 min (billing/WIP/waivers flow)

### Maintenance and update policy

- Every SOP has owner + review cycle date
- Monthly governance review:
  - stale pages
  - broken links
  - permission drift
- Quarterly operations review:
  - submittal/RFI cycle times
  - PPC trends
  - rework root causes
