# Operating Cadence (Weekly / Monthly)

Standard meeting and review rhythm for a small sprinkler subcontractor to keep execution predictable.

---

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Meeting | Attendees | Output |
|-----|---------|-----------|--------|
| Monday AM | Operations huddle | Owner, PM, super, shop, purchasing | 3-week constraints log |
| Tuesday | Design / submittal review | Design, PM | Submittal priorities |
| Wednesday | Financial pulse | Accounting, PM, owner | Billing, AR, cash look |
| Thursday | Field/shop sync | Super, shop, purchasing, PM | Material and manpower alignment |
| Friday | Risk close loop | Leadership | Top risks + owners |

---

## Monthly rhythm

| Window | Topic | Owner |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Week 1 | Month-end close and WIP | Accounting |
| Week 2 | Safety + quality review | Super + safety |
| Week 3 | Estimating hit rate and backlog quality | Estimating + owner |
| Week 4 | Process improvement / lessons learned | PMO + leadership |

---

## Cadence feedback loop

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  M[Meetings] --> D[Decisions]
  D --> A[Action owners]
  A --> V[Verify completion]
  V --> M
```
