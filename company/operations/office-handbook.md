# Office Handbook (Quick Start)

A practical quick-start for office staff: who does what, where information lives, and how to escalate.

---

## First things to know

| Topic | Standard |
|------|----------|
| Projects are the unit of execution | Every decision ties to a job number |
| Update systems daily | If it is not updated, it does not exist |
| Issues need owners and due dates | No orphan tasks |
| Escalate early | Small risks become expensive fast |

---

## Core handoffs

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  EST[Estimate/Bid] --> PM[PM/Design]
  PM --> PUR[Purchasing]
  PUR --> SH[Shop]
  SH --> FLD[Field]
  FLD --> ACCT[Accounting]
```

---

## Who to contact first

| Need | First contact |
|------|---------------|
| Billing or vendor payment | Accounting |
| Material lead-time risk | Purchasing |
| Submittal issue | Design + PM |
| Schedule risk in field | Superintendent + PM |
| Tool/system access | IT/Admin |
