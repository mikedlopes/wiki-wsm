# Role Onboarding Plans (30-60-90)

Use role-specific plans to accelerate ramp-up while keeping expectations realistic.

---

## Office roles

| Role | 30-day focus | 60-day focus | 90-day outcome |
|------|--------------|--------------|----------------|
| Accountant | Job cost flow, AP/AR basics | Billing cadence ownership | Independent month-end tasks |
| Estimator/Bidder | Takeoff process, scope review | Build complete estimate package | Lead bid with review |
| PM | Schedule, CO log, comms rhythm | Lead weekly OAC and internal reviews | Own project outcomes |
| Designer | Standards + calc workflow | Complete submittal package | Independent design release |
| Purchasing | Vendor map + PO controls | Manage lead-time risks | Own buyout on active projects |

---

## Operations roles

| Role | 30-day focus | 60-day focus | 90-day outcome |
|------|--------------|--------------|----------------|
| Shop manager/fab lead | SOP, QC hold points | Capacity planning | On-time release performance baseline |
| Superintendent | Site planning + safety controls | Run look-ahead cadence | Reliable inspection-ready execution |
