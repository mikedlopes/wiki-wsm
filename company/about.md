# About the Company

We are a specialty fire sprinkler subcontractor delivering **life-safety systems** for California projects. Our focus is on practical, code-compliant, constructible sprinkler solutions for tenant improvements and design-build work.

---

## What we do

| Service line | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Design-build sprinkler systems | End-to-end design, engineering, fabrication, install, testing |
| Tenant improvements | Fast-track modifications and phased occupancy support |
| New commercial construction | Full system delivery and turnover |
| Residential (where in scope) | Multi-family / mixed-use and code-required support |
| Service and deficiencies | NFPA 25 inspections, repairs, emergency response |

## Why clients choose us
