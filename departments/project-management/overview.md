## PM lifecycle

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  K[Kickoff] --> B[Baseline schedule & budget]
  B --> E[Execution]
  E --> C[Change management]
  C --> T[Testing & commissioning]
  T --> O[Closeout]
  O --> W[Warranty]
```
