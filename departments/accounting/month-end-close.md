## Close calendar (business days)

| BD | Task | Owner |
|----|------|-------|
| 0 | Lock payroll | Payroll |
| 1 | AP invoice cutoff communication | Accounting |
| 2 | Job cost freeze & ETC import | PM + Accounting |
| 3 | Bank reconciliations | Staff accountant |
| 4 | WIP & margin review meeting | Controller + Ops |
| 5 | Preliminary P&L / BS | Controller |
| 6 | Executive review packet | CFO |

---

## Close checklist (abbreviated)

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A[Subledgers tied] --> B[AR / AP reconciled]
  B --> C[Inventory / WIP]
  C --> D[Accruals]
  D --> E[Financial statements]
  E --> F[Board / bank package]
```
