# Service Department — Inspections, Deficiencies & Small Projects

Service delivers recurring **NFPA 25 inspections**, **deficiency repairs**, emergency calls, and small TI/service projects with fast dispatch and disciplined documentation.

---

## Service KPIs

| KPI | Target |
|-----|--------|
| On-time inspection completion | >= 98% |
| Deficiency quote turnaround | <= 3 business days |
| First-time fix rate | >= 85% |
| 24/7 emergency response (metro) | <= 2 hours |

---

## Service workflow

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  C[Customer request] --> D[Dispatch]
  D --> T[Tech visit]
  T --> R[Report + deficiency]
  R --> Q[Repair quote]
  Q --> A[Approval]
  A --> F[Fix + retest]
  F --> B[Bill + close]
```
